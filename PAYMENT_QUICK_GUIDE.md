# Quick Guide: Recording Payments

## 🎯 The Simple Answer

**When someone pays you back (or you pay someone back):**

1. Find their card in the People list
2. Tap **"Add Payment"** button
3. Enter the amount they paid
4. Tap **"Add Payment"**
5. Done! ✅

The app automatically:
- Updates "Paid" amount
- Updates "Remaining" amount  
- Updates progress bar
- Saves payment history

## 💡 Key Points

### ✅ DO
- Record each payment as it happens
- Add notes to remember context
- Check payment history by tapping the payment link
- Settle only when fully paid

### ❌ DON'T
- Don't settle before fully paid
- Don't forget to record payments
- Don't enter more than remaining amount

## 🎓 Real Example

**Scenario:** You lent ₹1000 to Rahul

**Day 1:** Create entry
- Total: ₹1000
- Paid: ₹0
- Remaining: ₹1000

**Day 5:** Rahul gives you ₹400
1. Tap "Add Payment"
2. Enter: 400
3. Tap "Add Payment"
- Result: Paid: ₹400, Remaining: ₹600

**Day 10:** Rahul gives you ₹600
1. Tap "Add Payment"
2. Enter: 600
3. Tap "Add Payment"
- Result: Paid: ₹1000, Remaining: ₹0

**Day 10:** Mark as complete
1. Tap "Settle"
2. Confirm
- Result: Entry moved to "Settled" tab

## ⚡ Quick Tips

1. **Partial Payments**: You can add as many payments as needed
2. **Progress Bar**: Visual indicator of how much is paid
3. **Payment History**: Always accessible by tapping the payment link
4. **Auto-Calculate**: App calculates remaining amount automatically
5. **Notes**: Add context to each payment for future reference

## 🆘 Need Help?

**Q: Where's the Add Payment button?**
A: On each person's card, below the progress bar

**Q: Can I add multiple payments?**
A: Yes! Add as many as needed until fully paid

**Q: How do I see all payments?**
A: Tap "💰 ₹XXX paid • View history" on the card

**Q: When should I tap Settle?**
A: Only when Remaining = ₹0 (fully paid)
